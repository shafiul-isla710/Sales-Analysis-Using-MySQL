# Sales-Analysis-Using-MySQL
## What I Did in This Project
Database Design: Created a database and designed three related tables: Sales, Customers, and Products

Data Insertion: Inserted sample customer, product, and sales records into the database.

Multiple Table Joins: Used INNER JOIN to combine sales, customer, and product information.

Subqueries: Identified products with sales amounts higher than the average sales amount.

Nested Aggregation: Identified the region with the highest total sales using nested aggregation queries.

Advanced Grouping: Analyzed total sales by region and product category.

HAVING Clause: Filtered grouped sales results based on aggregate conditions.

Subqueries in SELECT: Calculated total sales and average sales using a derived table.

Sales Analysis: Created queries to identify top customers and highest-value sales transactions.

Dashboard Preparation: Prepared monthly sales and customer-level summary queries for reporting and visualization.

## Project Objectives
Understand company sales performance.
Identify the highest-performing sales region.
Analyze sales by product category and region.
Identify top customers and high-value transactions.
Prepare structured sales data for business reporting.
